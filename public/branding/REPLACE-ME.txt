ADD YOUR BRAND ASSETS HERE
==========================

1. Replace logo.svg with your real logo (ideally a transparent-background SVG).
2. The website header uses /branding/logo.svg via src/components/SiteHeader.tsx.
3. For a PNG logo, save it as logo.png and change that one header path to /branding/logo.png.
4. Replace src/app/icon.svg with your square brand icon (favicon).
5. The theme colours and page styling live in src/app/globals.css.

The included SVG files are intentionally simple placeholders; they're not your final branding.
